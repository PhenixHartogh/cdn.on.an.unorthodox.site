Privacy Policy
Last Updated: 12 April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Where Is It?** ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is developed and operated by **Phenix Hartogh**.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at:
[WhereIsIt@phenixhartogh.com.au](mailto:WhereIsIt@phenixhartogh.com.au)

1. Information We Collect

When you use the App, we may collect the following types of information.

Account Information
When you create an account, we collect your email address through the authentication service Auth0. Authentication is handled entirely through Auth0.

Item and Location Data
The App allows you to create items and log where you placed them. The following information may be stored:

• Item names (for example: keys, wallet, laptop)
• Named locations you create (for example: kitchen bench, desk drawer)
• Photos you attach to item logs
• Time and date of item placements

This information is private and only visible to your account.

Device Information
We may collect limited device information such as:

• Device model
• Operating system version
• Basic diagnostic information

This information helps ensure the App functions correctly.

Photos
If you choose to attach photos to item logs, those images are stored with your item data.

Location Information
The App may allow you to log named locations you create, but it does not collect precise GPS location data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

• Provide and operate the App
• Allow you to track and locate your items
• Synchronise your data across devices
• Maintain and improve the functionality and reliability of the App

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Authentication

Account login and authentication are provided by **Auth0**, a third-party identity platform. When you log in, certain information such as your email address is processed by Auth0 according to their privacy policies.

4. Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by **Google AdSense**.

Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to display relevant advertisements. These technologies may collect certain information about your device or interactions with ads.

For more information about how Google uses data, please refer to Google's privacy policy.

5. Data Storage

User data is stored using the **Replit Database** infrastructure. The physical server location may vary depending on the hosting environment.

Your data may be stored indefinitely unless you request deletion.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing:

[WhereIsIt@phenixhartogh.com.au](mailto:WhereIsIt@phenixhartogh.com.au)

Account deletion requests will be processed within **60 days**.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of **5**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under this age.

If we become aware that personal information from a child under this age has been collected, we will take steps to delete it.

9. User Content

Users may upload photos and create item logs within the App. Users agree not to upload content that is illegal, harmful, abusive, or violates applicable laws.

The developer reserves the right to remove content that violates these rules.

10. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on the following third-party services:

• Auth0 (authentication services)
• Google AdSense (advertising services)
• Replit Database (data storage infrastructure)

These services may process limited information as required to provide their functionality.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When changes occur, the updated version will be posted with a revised “Last Updated” date.
