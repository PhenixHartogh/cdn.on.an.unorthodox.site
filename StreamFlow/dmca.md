# StreamFlow DMCA & Copyright Policy

**Last Updated:** April 12, 2026

StreamFlow respects the intellectual property rights of copyright holders and expects users of the Service to do the same. This DMCA and Copyright Policy explains how StreamFlow responds to copyright infringement claims relating to content accessible through the StreamFlow platform.

StreamFlow complies with applicable international copyright laws, including the **United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA)** where relevant.

---

# 1. Platform Overview

StreamFlow provides a platform that allows users to access **publicly available Free-to-Air television streams from around the world**.

StreamFlow **does not host, upload, broadcast, transmit, or control any television channels**.

Channel listings are **automatically loaded from publicly available sources**, and StreamFlow does not control the streams or broadcasts provided by third parties.

Users **cannot upload their own streams or playlists** to the StreamFlow platform.

# 3. Required Information for Copyright Notices

To help us process your request efficiently, your copyright notice should include:

1. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
2. Identification of the material or stream you believe is infringing, including the specific channel or listing within StreamFlow.
3. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use of the material is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
4. Your name, address, email address, and contact details.
5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate.
6. A statement made under penalty of perjury that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
7. Your physical or electronic signature.

Incomplete notices may delay the investigation process.

---

# 4. Investigation Process

When StreamFlow receives a copyright complaint, we will:

1. Review the submitted notice.
2. Investigate the claim to determine whether the reporting party holds the copyright to the material.
3. Complete the investigation **within 14 days** where reasonably possible.

After the investigation is completed, we will contact the reporting party with the results and discuss the appropriate action.

If our investigation determines that the reporting party holds the copyright to the identified material, StreamFlow will take the requested action where appropriate. This may include:

* Removing the channel listing
* Disabling access to the stream
* Blocking the stream within the platform

---

# 5. Counter-Notification Process

If you believe that material was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, you may submit a **counter-notification**.

Your counter-notice should include:

1. Identification of the material that was removed or disabled.
2. A statement that you believe the removal occurred as a result of mistake or misidentification.
3. Your name, address, and contact details.
4. A statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the appropriate courts.
5. Your physical or electronic signature.

Counter-notices should be submitted to:

**[dmca@streamflow.lol](mailto:dmca@streamflow.lol)**

# 6. Repeat Infringer Policy

StreamFlow maintains a policy of terminating access for **repeat copyright violators**.

Users who are found to violate copyright rules **three or more times** may have their accounts permanently banned.

---

# 7. False Claims

Submitting false copyright infringement notices or counter-notifications may have legal consequences.

Any person who knowingly misrepresents that material is infringing may be liable for damages under applicable copyright laws.

---

# 8. Limitation of Responsibility

Because StreamFlow **does not host or control television broadcasts**, we may not always be able to remove content directly from its original source.

In such cases, StreamFlow may remove or disable access to the channel listing within the platform where possible.
