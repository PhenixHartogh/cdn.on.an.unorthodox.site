# 1. Purpose of the Service

StreamFlow provides a platform that allows users to access **publicly available Free-to-Air television streams from around the world**.

StreamFlow does **not host, upload, broadcast, or control television channels**.

All broadcasts are provided by **third-party sources**.

Users must use the Service responsibly and in accordance with applicable laws.

# 2. Prohibited Activities

You agree **not to use StreamFlow to engage in any of the following activities**.

## 2.1 Security Violations

Users may not:

* Attempt to hack, exploit, or gain unauthorised access to the Service
* Interfere with the operation or security of StreamFlow
* Attempt to bypass authentication, CAPTCHA, or security protections
* Attempt to probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the platform

---

## 2.2 Reverse Engineering

Users may not:

* Reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any part of the StreamFlow platform
* Attempt to copy or replicate the StreamFlow software or infrastructure
* Attempt to discover the source code of the Service

---

## 2.3 Automated Access

Users may not:

* Use bots, automated scripts, or scraping tools
* Collect channel listings or platform data automatically
* Access the Service through automated means without permission

---

## 2.4 Commercial Exploitation

Users may not:

* Resell access to StreamFlow
* Offer StreamFlow as part of a commercial service
* Monetise or redistribute StreamFlow content without permission

---

## 2.5 Illegal Use

You may not use StreamFlow for any unlawful purposes, including but not limited to:

* Violating copyright laws
* Engaging in fraud or deceptive practices
* Distributing malware or harmful software
* Conducting activities that violate local, national, or international law
