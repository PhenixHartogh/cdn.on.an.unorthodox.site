# Data Deletion Instructions – NEPass

**Last updated:** 28 March 2026

## 1. Overview

NEPass is a private authentication service operated by **Phenix Hartogh**.

NEPass retains certain account and authentication records for security, auditing, and operational purposes. As a result, **NEPass does not permanently delete core account records** once they have been created.

However, users may request **account deactivation** and may delete certain types of data associated with their account.

This page explains:

* What data can be deleted
* What data cannot be deleted
* How to request account deactivation
* How to remove linked authentication providers
* How to request removal of authentication devices

---

## 2. Data That Users Can Delete

Users may remove certain data associated with their NEPass account.

This includes:

* Active login sessions
* Device sessions
* Linked social login providers
* Two-factor authentication devices or tokens

These items may be removed either:

* directly through NEPass account settings (where available), or
* by contacting the NEPass IT Helpdesk.

---

## 3. Data That Cannot Be Deleted

For security and auditing purposes, NEPass retains certain information indefinitely.

This includes:

* Core account records
* Authentication logs
* Audit logs
* Security logs
* Historical login records

These records are required to maintain the integrity and security of the authentication system.

Even if an account is deactivated, these records may remain stored.

---

## 4. Social Login Data

Users may unlink external identity providers from their NEPass account.

Supported providers may include:

* Apple
* Discord
* Facebook
* GitHub
* Google
* Telegram
* Twitter (X)

When a provider is unlinked:

* The provider identifier associated with the account is removed from active authentication records.
* Historical logs may still retain the **name of the provider used for authentication** (for example, "Facebook") for security auditing purposes.

---

## 5. Account Deactivation Requests

Users may request that their NEPass account be **deactivated**.

To request account deactivation, send an email to:

**[IT.Helpdesk@hartogh.net.au](mailto:IT.Helpdesk@hartogh.net.au)**

Your request must include:

* NEPass username
* Email address associated with the account
* Reason for the request
* Proof of identity documentation

Requests that do not include the required information may not be processed.

---

## 6. Identity Verification Requirements

To protect users from unauthorised deletion requests, NEPass requires identity verification before processing account deactivation requests.

Requests must include **certified identity documents** in one of the following formats:

**Option A**

* One government-issued **photo identification document**, and
* One government-issued **non-photo identification document**

**Option B**

* Two government-issued **non-photo identification documents**

All documents must be **certified by a person authorised under Justice Victoria guidelines**.

If acceptable proof of identity is not provided, the request **will not be processed**.

---

## 7. Processing Time

Account deactivation and data removal requests may take **up to 56 business days (Monday to Friday)** to process.

Processing times may vary depending on operational requirements or the complexity of the request.

---

## 8. Backups

NEPass performs **daily system backups**.

Backups are retained for **365 days**.

If an account is deactivated or certain data is removed, copies of that information may remain in backups until those backups expire and are automatically deleted.

---

## 9. Partial Data Removal

Users may request removal of specific authentication-related data without deactivating their account.

This may include:

* Removing linked social login providers
* Removing two-factor authentication devices or tokens
* Terminating active login sessions
* Removing remembered devices

Where available, these actions may also be performed directly by the user within the NEPass account settings.

---

## 10. Legal and Security Retention

Certain information may be retained indefinitely for legitimate operational purposes, including:

* Security monitoring
* Fraud prevention
* System auditing
* Compliance with internal security policies

Because NEPass operates as a secure authentication system, **permanent deletion of core account records is not supported**.

---
