## 4. Two-Factor Authentication

NEPass requires all users to configure at least **one form of two-factor authentication (2FA)**.

Supported authentication methods include:

* **Time-based One-Time Passwords (TOTP)**
* **Passkeys / WebAuthn authentication**
* **Static recovery tokens**

Users must configure either **TOTP or Passkeys**. Static tokens may be enabled optionally by the user.

Static tokens are considered less secure. If a static token is compromised and results in unauthorised access, the user may be held responsible for any resulting activity or damage.
